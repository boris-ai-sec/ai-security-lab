# Lab Scope

## In scope

- One controlled MoySklad product creation
- One principal Bitrix24 stage transition: `NEW → PREPARATION`
- Reconstruction of approved vs recorded invocation parameters
- Target response and separate fresh-read evidence
- Available MoySklad audit corroboration
- Bitrix history/timeline observability through the connected toolset
- Recovered result and final target-state evidence after a reported client-side interruption

## Lab questions

1. What evidence can establish that a reported state-changing action corresponded to an observed business-system state?
2. Can approved parameters be reconstructed and compared with the recorded invocation?
3. What additional evidence is available from the target business system?
4. What remains uncertain when target-system observability is incomplete?
5. What can be established after the operator temporarily loses the client-side acknowledgement path?

## Out of scope

- Penetration testing
- Production validation
- Full architecture or security assessment of FRIS
- General reliability benchmark
- Authorization-policy review
- Backend packet capture or server logs
- Exhaustive audit of all event/entity types
- Certification or readiness decision

## Evidence and judgment boundary

`Harness/tool result ≠ evidence interpretation ≠ finding ≠ human judgment ≠ public claim`

This package records observations and bounded interpretations. It does not issue a readiness decision.
